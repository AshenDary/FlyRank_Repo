## Where I Chose Real Over AI, and Why

- **Work screenshots (MemoAgent, LGU Risk Radar):** used real captures, not AI stand-ins, because the point of a case study is proving the thing actually works — a generated mockup would undercut the proof instead of supporting it.
- **About page photo:** will use a real photo, not a generated one, because the subject is me. A synthetic headshot would work against the direct, no-buzzwords voice the rest of the site is built on.
- **GitHub / LinkedIn / email icons:** chose a standard icon library over AI generation. These are recognizable, in some cases trademarked, marks — an AI reinterpretation risks looking slightly wrong, and a slightly-wrong logo reads worse than a correct one. Pulled from Lucide instead and recolored to match the kit.
- **Hero texture:** this is the one place AI generation made sense — it's abstract connective tissue, not proof of anything, so there's no "real" version to prefer over it.

## What I Rejected, and Why

Generated four grid-texture variants from one prompt. Picked "Subtle Technical Grid Composition" and rejected the other three because they read as busier and more visually active by comparison — more line density competing for attention instead of sitting quietly behind hero text. The chosen version stays restrained enough to support the kit's "calm, work stays loudest" mood instead of competing with it.
